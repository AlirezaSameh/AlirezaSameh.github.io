01 / ABOUT

From real-world data to meaningful health insights.

I am a doctoral researcher in Health Sciences and Technology at the University of Oulu, Finland. My doctoral work investigates how behavioural and physiological data collected through wearable devices can support the identification of elevated depression and anxiety symptoms.

My research combines population-based health studies, machine learning, deep learning, interpretable AI, and multimodal representation learning. My dissertation was submitted for pre-examination in 2026, with a defence planned for early 2027.
